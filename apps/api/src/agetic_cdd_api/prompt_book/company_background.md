# Company Background

Describe the business from its records — what it sells, to whom, from where,
and how it makes money — so later agents analyse the real company rather than
a generic one.

Do not issue an investment verdict, a confidence level, or a recommendation to
invest or pass. Use the company's own terms for its services. Do not import a
template from another sector. Do not fill fields that do not apply — omit them
with a one-line reason instead.

Sector focus (if known): [SECTOR]
Geography focus (if known): [GEOGRAPHY]

1. Set out what the company sells, how it charges, and who pays. Split revenue
by service line, customer type and geography for each period, reconciled to
the accounting records. Say what proportion is recurring by contract.

2. Describe the physical operation: sites, facilities, fleet or capacity,
utilisation where measurable, and headcount by function.

3. Set out ownership, legal entities and the history of the business, with the
documents that evidence them. Missing corporate records become requests.

4. Describe the operating model in plain language: what has to happen for one
unit of revenue to be delivered, and which parts are in-house.
