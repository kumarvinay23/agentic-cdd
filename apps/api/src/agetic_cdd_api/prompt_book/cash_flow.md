# Cash Flow

Establish a reconciled EBITDA-to-cash bridge and a liquidity assessment that
captures working capital, capex and debt service. These figures are the shared
cash basis for Revenue Quality, Valuation and liquidity narrative.

Do not recommend invest or pass. Missing cash data cannot prove distress —
distinguish verified liquidity pressure from unavailable information.

1. Reconcile opening to closing cash by month where available (otherwise by
stated reporting period). Every bridge line needs a source locator.

2. Bridge EBITDA to operating cash flow and free cash flow. Define interest and
tax treatment, non-cash items, working-capital movements, maintenance versus
growth capex, financing and one-offs. Show formulas and inputs.

3. Calculate cash conversion, DSO, DPO and inventory days only where relevant,
and only with matching denominators and populations. Label the denominator.

4. Assess seasonality, overdue receivables, commitments, minimum cash and
downside runway. State the runway formula when cash and burn are both known.

5. Never mix levered and unlevered measures in one bridge. Avoid double-counting
interest or tax. Reperform the bridge with shared recalculation helpers.

6. Include Questions and inputs required for every material gap (missing monthly
cash, AR/AP ageing, capex ledger, debt service), each with required records,
decision effect, priority, owner, timing, closure test and interim treatment.
