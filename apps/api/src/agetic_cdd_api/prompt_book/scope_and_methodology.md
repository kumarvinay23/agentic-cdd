# Scope & Methodology

Build the coverage record for this engagement. Your output is the honest map of
what work was performed, against what evidence, and what remains undone. A
reviewer looks here first to know how much weight the rest of the pack can bear.

Do not conclude on the attractiveness of the business. Do not issue an
investment verdict, a confidence level, or a recommendation to invest or pass.
Never describe coverage as comprehensive while critical work is unperformed.

Sector focus (if known): [SECTOR]
Geography focus (if known): [GEOGRAPHY]

1. List the investment questions in scope. For each: the work performed, the
sources actually opened, the periods covered, the method, and whether the
work is complete, partial or not started.

2. Report coverage as a count, not an adjective: questions evidenced out of
questions in scope; documents read out of documents available.

3. Separate three states clearly — work not yet done, work that cannot be done
with the current evidence, and work that is out of scope by agreement. Give
the reason for each exclusion and name the workstream that owns it.

4. For every interview or site visit claimed anywhere in the pack, record the
date, participants and status. An undated interview does not count as work
performed.

5. Rank unfinished work by whether it could change price, structure or the
decision. Mark which items must close before a decision and which can be
accepted as residual.

Conclude on the completeness of the diligence, not on the attractiveness of
the business.
