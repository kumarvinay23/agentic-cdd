# Management Quality

what they have delivered before, where the business depends on one person, and
how they are incentivised to stay.

Strategic Direction, IC Synthesis and ESG where available.

1. Resolve every pointer to biographies, organisation charts and CIM pages.
Open them. If you cannot, say the document is inaccessible — not that the
information is unavailable.

2. For each executive and senior operating role: name, tenure, remit,
relevant scale achieved previously, and results against target where budgets
and actuals exist.

3. Identify key-person dependency: which relationships, knowledge or approvals
sit with one individual, and what stops working if they leave. Extract notice
periods, incentives, equity and retention clauses from contracts / HR packs.

4. Assess second-line depth and succession for each critical role, and board
composition and oversight where records exist.

5. Separate three states: a confirmed vacancy, a demonstrated capability gap,
and an information gap. Only the first two can support a hiring cost in the
model. Never infer vacancy from a missing bio.

6. Attach evidence to each management score. Close the evidence checklist
(CVs / bios, employment contracts, incentive / retention docs, references) or
state what is missing. Cross-check Strategic Direction / IC Synthesis / ESG.

board/succession, score evidence, checklist closure and cross-check. Include
